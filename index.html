Known limits of these backtests

  • Ratings use only games before each week, and weights only earlier seasons. Lines are closing lines (or your downloaded snapshots), which matches betting near kickoff.
  • Wind unders: the long record used actual game-time wind; the 2021–2026 check with archived near-kickoff forecasts (what you would have had) gave the same result, 58.9%. Forecasts from 1–3 days out did not hold up, so the board only marks an under as a bet within 12 hours of kickoff with a forecast under 6 hours old.
  • Injury data is each week's final report (out by Friday or Saturday): fine for bets near kickoff, not for Tuesday bets. The full stats line also knew each game's actual starting QB; it is context only and not in the projection.
  • 2013–2026 was used heavily while building the model, and 2000–2012 has now been examined once. The clean test is model v1.0's live record from Nov 24, 2026 on, saved in the encrypted prediction log.
  • Ranges allow for good and bad seasons (season-clustered), since bets within a season are not independent.

How the projection is built

How picks are made. A game is a bet when the special-teams adjustment plus any news you enter moves the line 0.5+ points from the market, and your best book's line is no worse than the market. Stakes are your Kelly fraction × an assumed 2% edge ÷ the payout, capped at 2% of bankroll per bet, 3% per game and 10% per week (all stakes are scaled down together if the week's total would exceed it). A line or price at your book counts as worse than the market when its value is lower than the market line at −110, so a half point worse at a better price can still qualify. The cover odds come from a smooth key-number model (exact margins of 3, 7, 10 and so on weighted by how close the game is expected to be) and are used to pick your best line, not to filter bets.

Projection = market + special teams + your news. The special-teams term is net special-teams EPA per game (kicking, punting, returns, coverage) for each team, weighted points per point of difference. That weight is re-learned each season. The full stats line is shown for context only (model v1.0 removed it from the projection), because walk-forward tests found it adds nothing beyond the close.

Full stats line (34 factors). Weekly ridge regressions rate every offense and defense, adjusted for opponents, on EPA, success rate, early-down EPA, turnover-free EPA, pass EPA, explosive-play rate and sack rate. Recent games count most. On top of that: special teams, interception and fumble rates, pass rate over expected, penalties, QB EPA and CPOE versus each team's baseline, injury reports weighted by snap share and position, home field, rest, time zones and weather.

Weather. AccuWeather forecasts for each outdoor stadium (day forecast for early kickoffs, night forecast for late ones). Wind of 10+ mph shades the expected total down 1.5 points, because closing totals ran 1.3–2 points high in that wind. Lower totals mean more games land exactly on 3 (20.6% vs 14.6% at a 3-point line), and the cover odds account for that. Cold games with a dome or warm-weather road team are a factor in the stats line.

Injuries. Each player listed Out (×1.0), Doubtful (×0.9) or Questionable (×0.15) is weighted by his snap share over his last six games, then by position-group values fitted on 2013–2025: about 1.3 points for a full-time WR, 1.0 for a TE, 0.8 for a LB, 0.5 for a DB, and smaller for linemen. QBs are handled separately through the starter dropdown.

Cover odds come from real final-margin distributions at each spread, split by low and normal totals. A fair line is always 50/50. Calibration check: picks the model rated 53.8% covered 54.4%, and those rated 56.7% covered 58.8%.
